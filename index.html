Worker profiles

工人画像：我们怎样排岗Worker profiles: how we place people

员工以墨西哥籍为主，岗位分布均衡。每位员工我们都持续记录四个维度，排岗依据画像而不是随机分配。Our workforce is mostly Mexican and evenly spread across roles. We track four dimensions for every worker and place by profile, never at random.

性格Temperament细心、主动、沉稳，适合什么节奏的岗位Careful, proactive or steady, and what pace suits them
优势Strengths体能、手速、专注、语言沟通Stamina, hand speed, focus, communication
短板Gaps需要避开的岗位，或需要带教的环节Roles to avoid, or skills to coach
时间安排Availability可上班时段、能否夜班和加班Shifts they can cover, nights and overtime
