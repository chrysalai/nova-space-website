Worker profiles

工人画像：我们怎样排岗Worker profiles: how we place and schedule

员工以墨西哥籍为主，岗位分布均衡。每位员工都有一份持续更新的画像，排岗和排班都以画像为依据。Our workforce is mostly Mexican and evenly spread across roles. Every worker has a living profile, and both placement and scheduling follow it.

01性格Temperament细心、主动、沉稳，适合什么节奏Careful, proactive, steady
02优势Strengths体能、手速、专注、语言沟通Stamina, speed, focus, language
03短板Gaps要避开的岗位或要带教的环节Roles to avoid, skills to coach
04时间习惯Time habits一周哪几天、哪个时段一定能来，能否夜班Which days and hours they can always work
05出勤可靠性Reliability是否常请假，是否每天都想上班How often they take leave, how much they want to work
员工每周出勤画像Weekly attendance profile先记录每个人一定能来的日子，再据此排班。We record the days each person can always work, then schedule from that.

画像回答"这个人擅长什么"，能力模型回答"这个岗位需要什么"，两者对齐就是排岗。The profile says what a person is good at; the model says what a station needs. Matching the two is placement.

以上为示例。员工个人画像仅供内部排班使用，不对外公开。Examples only. Individual profiles are used internally for scheduling and are never shared.
